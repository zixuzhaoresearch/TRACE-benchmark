The TRACE benchmark builds on the **Yelp Open Dataset**. We do **not** redistribute Yelp-owned content. This file documents what is and isn't included, and why.

## What is **not** included

| Component | Why |
|---|---|
| **Verbatim review text** | Yelp ToS §4.A: cannot publicly display reviews. |
| **LLM-generated quotations of reviews** | Even paraphrased quotes ≥90 fuzzy similarity are arguably derivative works of Yelp content (§5 Ownership). We replace them with `[Q:N]` placeholders backed by positional metadata. |
| Tip text, user account data | Yelp ToS §4.A. |
| Bulk POI listings without provenance | Would constitute "creating a competing local-listings DB" (§4.A). |

To obtain review text, see `scripts/reconstruct_kb.py` — it pulls text from your own copy of `yelp_academic_dataset_review.json` (you must download it after accepting the Yelp ToS) and rebuilds both the KB and the dialogue text.

## Quote-masking design

The `quote_spans` metadata is purposefully designed to contain **no Yelp word content**:

- **Exact verbatim** quotes (≈86% of cases): stored as `(review_id, [start, end])` — three integers + an ID.
- **Near-verbatim** quotes (≈11% of cases): stored as `(review_id, [start, end], edits)`. The `edits` list contains:
  - `ins_p`/`rep_p`: insert/replace using whitelisted punctuation only (`. , ; : ! ? ' " - / ( ) [ ] { } whitespace`). No alphanumeric content.
  - `ins_r`/`rep_r`: insert/replace by referencing another review span (`rid, c, d`). The actual word content is fetched from the user's hydrated Yelp data, never from our metadata.

Concretely, for any LLM-substituted alphanumeric content, the metadata stores **only positional pointers** into the corpus. A reader of our distribution who has not also accepted the Yelp ToS cannot reconstruct any Yelp word. This mirrors the "ship the IDs, hydrate locally" pattern used by every Yelp-derived academic benchmark.

The remaining ≈3% of LLM-quoted content (substantive paraphrase or non-quote regex artifacts) is left as plain text — these are demonstrably original LLM output that does not retrieve back to a single review span at ≥90 fuzzy similarity.

## Yelp Dataset Terms of Use — key clauses (Feb 2021)

Authoritative source: <https://www.yelp.com/dataset>. The clauses below are summarized; the full agreement controls.

- **§3 License grant**: royalty-free, non-exclusive, **non-sublicensable**, **non-transferable**, **revocable**, for **non-commercial use only**.
- **§3 Publication**: prior to public presentation involving the Data and the Yelp brand name, results must be submitted to Yelp for review (5 business days).
- **§4.A**: cannot publicly display the Data, **especially reviews and other user generated content**.
- **§4.B**: no commercial use.
- **§4.E**: cannot redistribute summaries or metrics — *except* disclosures necessary for academic publication.
- **§4.H**: cannot rent, lease, sell, transfer, assign, or sublicense any part of the Data.
- **§10 Term**: 12 months, terminable at any time; on termination must delete all copies.

## How to comply when using TRACE

By downloading and using TRACE, you acknowledge that:

1. You will obtain your own copy of the Yelp Open Dataset (and accept Yelp's ToS) before hydrating review text.
2. You will use the resulting hydrated KB for **non-commercial academic research only**.
3. You will not redistribute review text.
4. If your institution has commercial interests, you will obtain a separate authorization from Yelp before using the data in production.
